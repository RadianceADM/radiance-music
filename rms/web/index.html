Set up your Radiance Music Server

Your own server for Radiance Music. It splits songs for you (drums, bass, other, vocals, and every word's time), keeps your library, and lets the friends you invite play from it, split on it and join your listening parties. Nothing here is public: only people you invite can use it.

What happens next

You get your own join link (and its QR) for Radiance Music on your phone and computer. Then you invite friends one by one, each with a link of their own that works once. You can take any of it back.
